ArquiusBot

ArquiusBot is a Discord moderation and utility bot operated for fan communities. It is a private bot and is not available for public invite. This page exists for Discord ToS purposes.

Features

Moderation
  • Timed channel bans, reaction bans, and full server bans.
  • Channel locking and bulk message deletion with a per-author breakdown in the mod log
Moderation Logging
  • Logs edited and deleted messages (including attachments) to a private mod log channel
  • Logs member joins and leaves with account age and server tenure
  • Logs nickname changes, role additions and removals, Discord timeouts, bans and unbans
  • Logs channel creation, modification, and deletion; role creation and deletion
Role Management
  • Automatically restores a member's roles when they rejoin after leaving
  • Reaction-based self-assignable colour roles (one at a time per member)
Reddit Integration
  • Monitors r/homestuck and posts qualifying submissions (score threshold, no NSFW) to a Discord channel.
Utility Commands
  • /roll — dice roller supporting NdF+M notation
  • /latex — renders a LaTeX equation to an image
  • /info — shows account creation date, join date, and roles for a member
  • And more!

Privacy Policy

ArquiusBot does not collect personal data from server members. For moderation purposes, Discord user IDs and role IDs are stored temporarily in a local database on the bot operator's private server, only for as long as needed to carry out the relevant action (e.g. lifting a timed ban or restoring roles after a member rejoins), after which they are automatically deleted.

Edited and deleted message content is posted to a private moderation log channel inside the same Discord server.
