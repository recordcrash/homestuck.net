What Happened to the Homestuck Discord?

For the latest updates, follow the Reddit thread and the Homestuck subreddit.

What happened

On February 18, 2026, the Homestuck Discord server was taken down following a DMCA claim. The server is only disabled, not deleted, and we are in the process of trying to bring it back.

What we know

Unfortunately, not much. Tickets are open, but Discord Support has been very slow to respond to our queries. We don't yet know who filed the DMCA claim or what specific content it targeted.

Where to find updates

Updates will be posted here as we learn more:

  • This Reddit thread tracking the situation
  • The r/homestuck subreddit in general

A note on the server URL

The discord.gg/homestuck invite link has been taken over by an unrelated party. Do not use it. If the server comes back, a new invite URL will be announced through the subreddit. If the DMCA is demonstrated to be unfounded, I suppose Discord might also give it back to us.
